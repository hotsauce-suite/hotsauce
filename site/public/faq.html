About Hotsauce

Who is Hotsauce for?

Teams of roughly 5 to 50 people who want their documents and meeting notes on infrastructure they control, without paying per seat. You need one person who is comfortable following a setup guide. After that, everything is managed in the browser.

Is it really free?

Yes. The code is free under AGPL-3.0 and every feature is included. You pay your own hosting (about $6 a month on Fly.io, or $13–15 on Cloudflare) and, if you turn them on, your own Anthropic, Deepgram and storage usage.

What does AGPL-3.0 mean for my company?

You can run Hotsauce for your team, change it, and keep using it, with no fees. If you offer a modified version to people outside your organization over a network, you have to share your changes under the same license. Using it internally doesn’t trigger that.

Can I try it before installing?

Yes. The live demo is the real app with made-up sample data, right in your browser. Nothing you do there is saved.

Which apps are included?

Today, Docs, Meetings (with booking links), Tasks, Notes, Files, Whiteboard, People, Forms, Tables, Links, Check-ins, Clips, Dictate and the daily briefing, plus a Mac app, a Chrome extension and a built-in MCP server for AI agents. The help center has a page for each.

Your data

Where does my data go?

Your files live on your server, or in your GitHub organization if you choose that. Audio is sent to Deepgram for transcription and text to Anthropic for notes, using your own accounts. Clips and kept meeting audio stay on your server’s disk, or in your own Cloudflare R2 or S3-compatible bucket.

What format are my files in?

Docs, meeting notes, tasks, notes, tables and links are plain Markdown files in a git repository, with a full history of every change. Open them in any editor, back them up, or take them anywhere. The database is only a search index that Hotsauce can rebuild from the files.

Can admins read my personal space?

No. Your personal space is visible only to you. There is no admin override for reading it.

Is it backed up?

With storage set up, the server backs itself up every day to your own bucket, and every change to a file is kept in its git history. See Backups.

Can people outside my company see shared links?

Only if an admin turns that on. By default a shared link opens only for signed-in members. See Share links and pages.

AI and keys

Do I need the AI keys?

No. Docs, search, Notes, Files, Whiteboard, Tables and Clips recording work without them. A Deepgram key turns on transcripts and Dictate. An Anthropic key turns on AI meeting notes, Ask, summaries and clean-up. See what each key turns on.

How much does the AI cost?

For a team of ten, typically a few dollars a month each for Anthropic and Deepgram, billed by them to your own accounts. Admins can see each person’s assistant use in Settings and set a monthly limit.

Does a bot join my meetings?

No. Meetings records from your own browser tab, the Chrome extension for Google Meet, or the Mac app, so there’s no extra attendee in the call.

Can I use Claude with my Hotsauce?

Yes. Make a token in Settings and connect Claude Code or Claude desktop to the built-in MCP server. It sees exactly what you can see. Here’s how.

Using it

How do people sign in?

With Google, through a free Clerk application the setup wizard walks you through. The server only lets in email addresses from the domains you choose, plus anyone you invite by email, who signs in with a code.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Open it in your phone’s browser and add it to your home screen. It then opens full screen, sends notifications, and records voice notes. Here’s how.

Is there a Mac app? Windows?

There’s a Mac app for Dictate, recording any call and the suite in its own window. On Windows and Linux, use the browser and install it as an app from Chrome or Edge.

I’m not getting notifications.

Open Settings and turn on Notifications on this device on each phone and computer you use, and allow notifications when the browser asks. Then press Send test notification to check. An iPhone needs Hotsauce on its home screen first. See Settings and notifications.

Transcripts spell names wrong.

Add the names to your word list in Dictate → Words. Meetings, Dictate and Clips all use it, and your team can share one list.

Running it

How do I install it?

Click Deploy to Cloudflare, ask your AI coding agent to follow hotsauce.work/install.md, or run it on Fly.io or Docker. See the three ways.

How do I update it?

On Fly.io it can update itself from Settings → Organization → Updates. With Docker, run docker compose pull and docker compose up -d. Your files and settings stay on the server.

Can I put my own brand on it?

Yes. Name the workspace, add your logo, and pick colors, fonts and a default look in the setup wizard or Settings → Organization. Every app follows.

Where do I report a bug or ask for a feature?

Open an issue on GitHub. Inside your own Hotsauce, Send feedback goes to whoever runs it.
