Help / The apps

Docs

Docs is where your team writes things down: plans, specs, handbooks, notes. Every doc is a Markdown file, and every save is kept in its history.

Try it in the live demo →Sample data, nothing is saved.

Write a doc

  1. Open Docs and pick a space: Team for everyone, or your own.
  2. Choose a folder, then New doc.
  3. Write. The editor shows Markdown as you type: # for a heading, - for a list, - [ ] for a checkbox.
  4. Flip to the reading view to see it the way others will.

Present a doc as slides

Any doc can be a slide deck. Put a line with only --- between slides, or just use ## headings: a doc with no --- starts a new slide at each one. Under a line starting Notes:, write what you want to say; only you see it. Because a deck is an ordinary doc, it keeps its history, search finds it, and Ask can read it.

  • Present. Press the screen button at the top of a doc (or ⌘ K, then Present). The slides fill the screen in your team’s fonts and colours. Use → or Space for the next slide and ← for the one before, type a number and Enter to jump there, and Esc to stop.
  • Presenter view. With a second screen, Present opens it by itself; otherwise press P, or choose Present with presenter view in ⌘ K. You see this slide, the next one, your notes, a timer and the clock, and the slides open in a second window: drag it to the projector and press F for full screen. In the Mac app the slides go full screen on the other display by themselves. Closing the slides window ends the show.
  • Laser and black screen. L turns on a red laser dot that follows your pointer, and the audience sees it too. B blacks out the screen; any key brings it back.
  • Draft slides. In ⌘ K, Draft slides from this doc asks the AI for a deck with speaker notes. It is saved as a new doc next to yours, called “… slides”, and your doc stays as it was. Needs an Anthropic key.
  • PDF. Download slides as PDF in ⌘ K saves one wide page per slide, without your notes, to send ahead or hand out.

Good to know

  • Working together. Several people can edit the same doc. Their changes merge when each person saves.
  • Offline. If the connection drops, your edits are kept on this device and the doc says Saved on this device. They’re sent by themselves when you’re back. See With no connection.
  • History. Open a doc’s history to see every version, who made it, and bring an old one back.
  • Links between docs. Type [[ and start typing a title. Links keep working when a doc is renamed or moved.
  • Stars. Star the docs you use most to keep them at the top, and on Today.
  • Live task lists. Drop a list of tasks into a page; ticking them off there updates Tasks.
  • Import. Bring in Markdown, Word and HTML files.
  • Search covers every space you can see.
  • Share a read-only link to a doc. See Share links and pages.
  • Word and PDF. Press Share, then Download as Word or Download as PDF. The file keeps the headings, lists, tables, images and links, in your team’s fonts and colours, with your logo on the first page. Live task lists come out as their tasks at that moment, and whiteboards as a picture. Meeting notes have the same two items. In the Mac app, File → Export… (⌘ ⇧ E) does the same and asks where to save it.
What it needs

Nothing extra. Docs works the moment the server is up.
