The apps

  • TodayYour day on one page: meetings, tasks, to-dos and changes.
  • DocsWrite and organize your team’s documents, with full history.
  • MeetingsRecord calls, get a transcript, and get clean notes written for you.
  • Booking linksShare a link so people can book a time that’s really free.
  • TasksA fast task list: type it the way you’d say it.
  • NotesQuick notes and checklists on colored cards.
  • FilesUpload, preview and share files, and search inside them.
  • BoardsDraw together live on hand-drawn boards.
  • PeopleEveryone you meet with, built from your calendar and notes.
