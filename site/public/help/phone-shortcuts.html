Phone shortcuts

Go from your phone’s Home Screen straight to a voice note, a new task, saving a link, Ask or Ramble, with no menus on the way.

There are five shortcuts:

  • Voice note starts recording at once.
  • New task opens the quick add box in Tasks.
  • Save link saves the link you copied to Links. Tap Paste and save, or paste the link into the box.
  • Ask opens the assistant.
  • Ramble starts listening at once. Say your tasks out loud and Hotsauce adds them.

On Android

  1. Install Hotsauce on your Home Screen from Chrome (see Install it).
  2. Press and hold the Hotsauce icon. The shortcuts appear above it.
  3. Tap one to go straight there. To give one its own icon, drag it onto your Home Screen.

Some phones show only the first four shortcuts, so Ramble may be missing. If you installed Hotsauce before the shortcuts were added, they can take a day to show up. To see them now, remove the app and add it again.

On iPhone

iPhone doesn’t show shortcuts when you hold a web app’s icon. Instead, you make each one in Apple’s Shortcuts app. Then it can go on your Home Screen, in the Shortcuts widget or on the Action Button.

  1. In Hotsauce, open Settings → Apps → Phone shortcuts, pick iPhone and tap Copy next to the shortcut you want.
  2. Open the Shortcuts app and tap +. Add the Open URLs action and paste the address into it.
  3. Name the shortcut, for example “New task”.
  4. Put it where you want it. To make it an icon, choose Add to Home Screen from the shortcut’s menu. You can also add the Shortcuts widget, or open the iPhone’s Settings → Action Button, choose Shortcut and pick it.

A shortcut opens Hotsauce in Safari, not in the Home Screen app, so sign in once in Safari too. For voice notes and Ramble, allow the microphone when Safari asks. To stop it asking every time, open the iPhone’s Settings → Apps → Safari → Microphone and choose Allow.

Save to Hotsauce from the Share button

  1. In the Shortcuts app, make a new shortcut. Open its details (ⓘ) and turn on Show in Share Sheet, receiving URLs.
  2. Add URL Encode on the Shortcut Input.
  3. Add Text. Paste the share address from Settings → Apps → Phone shortcuts, then add the encoded text right after it.
  4. Add Open URLs and name the shortcut “Save to Hotsauce”.
  5. In Safari, tap Share and pick Save to Hotsauce.

On a Mac

In the Mac app, right-click the Hotsauce icon in the Dock, or click the Hotsauce icon in the menu bar, and choose New Task, Save Link, Ask or Ramble Tasks. Save Link uses the link you copied, so you just tap Save to Links.

What it needs

Nothing extra. Ask needs the AI key, and voice notes and Ramble need the speech key, both added by an admin.
