Chrome extension

The Chrome extension records Google Meet calls without a bot, and saves any web page to Links.

Install it

Open Meetings → Chrome extension in Hotsauce and follow the steps there. It shows the right install for your workspace.

Take notes in Google Meet

  1. Join a call in Google Meet.
  2. Click the extension (or press ⌥ ⇧ H to open it) and choose Take notes.
  3. Your notes and the live transcript open in Hotsauce. Stop when the call ends.

No bot joins your call, and you don’t need Chrome’s “share tab audio” dialog.

Save links

On any page, click the extension and choose Save to Hotsauce, or right-click a page or a link. A ✓ on the icon means it’s saved in Links.
