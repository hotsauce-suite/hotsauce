Help / The apps

Share links and pages

Share a read-only copy of something with people who don’t use Hotsauce, or publish a web page an agent made.

Share a read-only link

  1. Open a doc or meeting note and choose Share (in a note, use the link button).
  2. Hotsauce makes a link and copies it, and tells you who can open it.
  3. Turn the link off from the same menu whenever you like. It stops working at once.

By default only signed-in members can open links. An admin can let people outside the organization open them too, in Settings → Organization.

Published pages

Claude and other agents can publish an HTML page to your workspace, like a report or a dashboard. Find them under Pages in the Docs sidebar, with every version kept. Pages run in a sealed frame with no access to your account.
