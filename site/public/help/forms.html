Help / The apps

Forms

Forms collects answers from your team or from anyone with the link. Answers land in Hotsauce, where you can read, summarize and download them.

Try it in the live demo →Sample data, nothing is saved.

Make a form

  1. Open Forms and make a new form.
  2. Add questions: short and long answers, email, choices, dropdowns, ratings, dates and file uploads.
  3. Make a question appear only after a certain earlier answer, if you need to.
  4. Choose who can answer: your team only, or anyone with the link.
  5. Share the link. Close the form when you’re done collecting.

Read the answers

  • A summary, each answer on its own, a table, or a CSV download.
  • Open as a table shows answers in Tables, always up to date.
  • Post new answers to a team chat room, if your admin added one.
What it needs

Forms open to the public need a free Cloudflare Turnstile key. An AI summary of the answers needs an Anthropic key.
