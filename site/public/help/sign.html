Help / The apps

Sign

Sign turns a PDF in Files into a signed, sealed copy. You place the fields, send it to the people who sign (inside or outside your team), and Hotsauce saves the signed PDF and an audit certificate next to the original.

Try it in the live demo →Sample data, nothing is saved.

Get a PDF ready

  1. Open Files, find the PDF, and choose Send for signature from its menu.
  2. Click a field type at the top (Signature, Initials, Date signed, Text or Checkbox), then click the page where it goes.
  3. Drag a field to move it, or drag its corner to resize it. From the keyboard, press Enter on a field type to put one in the middle of page 1, then use the arrow keys to move it, Alt and the arrow keys to resize it, and Delete to remove it.
  4. Change its name if you like. If you're the only one signing, press Sign it.

Send it to other people

  1. Under Who signs, change or remove yourself, and press Add a person for each other signer: a name and an email. They don't need an account.
  2. Set someone to Gets a copy if they only need the signed PDF at the end.
  3. With two or more signers, tick Sign one after another to have them sign in the list's order; otherwise everyone can sign at once.
  4. Pick a person under Fields for, then place their fields. Everyone who signs needs a signature field.
  5. Press Send. Each person gets an email with their own link. If your server has no email set up, Hotsauce shows each link for you to copy and send yourself.

Follow who has signed

  • The envelope page lists each person: link sent, opened, signed, declined, or waiting for their turn.
  • Press New link to make a fresh link for someone (their old one stops working), or Email again to send it to them. A link works for 30 days.
  • Typed someone's address wrong, or misspelled their name? Press Change beside them, before they sign. Their old link stops working, and if it's their turn the new address gets a fresh link. The change is kept in the audit trail.
  • If you sign too, press Sign it when it's your turn.
  • If someone declines, nobody else can sign it, and you get an email with their reason.
  • When the last person signs, everyone on it, copies included, is emailed the signed PDF and its certificate, and both are saved in Files.

Send it again

Press Copy to a new draft on any envelope you've sent, finished or not. You get a new draft with the same PDF, people and fields, with nothing filled in, ready to change and send. The original stays as it is. This is handy after someone declines.

Signing from an emailed link

The person opens the link, reads the PDF, presses Start signing, fills in their fields and agrees to sign electronically. They can press Decline instead and say why. The link is only for them: the audit trail records that they signed from their email link, with the time, address and browser.

Sign it

  1. Tick the box that you agree to sign electronically.
  2. Click each field. For a signature or initials, type your name or draw it with a mouse, trackpad or finger.
  3. Press Finish signing. Hotsauce stamps the fields onto the PDF and seals it.

What you get

  • The signed PDF, saved in Files beside the original. Its digital seal means Adobe Reader and other PDF readers show if anyone changes it later.
  • An audit certificate, also in Files: each step, who did it and when, the device and address it came from, and the fingerprints of the original and signed PDFs.
  • The audit trail on the envelope page. Each event is chained to the one before it, and the page says whether the chain still checks out.

Envelopes are private to you. On the list, the All, To sign and Signed tabs sort them.

What it needs

Nothing extra to sign. The server makes its own seal key the first time it signs and keeps it with your data. To email signing links and the finished copies, add a free Resend key (RESEND_API_KEY) and the address to send from (MAIL_FROM); without them you copy the links yourself.
