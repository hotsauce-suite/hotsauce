Help / The apps

Meetings

Meetings takes notes for you. Record a call, jot a few notes as you go, and when it ends Hotsauce writes clean notes from your jottings and the transcript. No bot joins your call.

Try it in the live demo →Sample data, nothing is saved.

Take notes on a call

  1. Start recording in one of three ways: from Meetings in your browser, with the Chrome extension in Google Meet, or from the Mac app for any call app.
  2. Pick a template if you like: a customer call, an interview or a stand-up.
  3. Jot a few notes while you talk. The transcript fills in live beside them.
  4. Stop when the call ends. Hotsauce writes the notes, and matches speakers to the names on the invite.

On a Mac, the Mac app notices calls by itself: when Zoom, Teams, Slack, FaceTime or a browser call such as Google Meet in Chrome starts, a card at the top right of the screen says Meeting detected with one button, Take notes. Click it and the notes start, with your mic and the call’s sound. Nothing records until you click. macOS asks once for the Microphone and once for System Audio Recording; after that there are no more prompts.

It also asks for calendar meetings. About a minute before a calendar meeting with other people or a call link, it shows Take notes for “the meeting”? Click it to join the call and start taking notes in one go, or choose Just take notes if you’re joining another way. Either way the notes start by themselves. If you skip it and join the call anyway, the offer to take notes on the call replaces it, named for the meeting, so you only ever see one.

More in Meetings

  • Search at the top of Meetings finds a meeting by its name or by who was there.
  • Ask about this meeting, or Ask across meetings to search everything ever said: “What did Pat say about the budget?”
  • Agent brief: a page about what you’re working on, written from your meetings and tasks, that you can hand to an AI tool.
  • Action items turn into tasks in one click.
  • Reminders a minute before each call, once Google Calendar is connected. On a Mac, one click joins the call and starts the notes.
  • Voice notes from your phone: record a thought, a call or a talk. See Dictate and voice notes.
  • Import from Granola brings your old meeting notes in.
  • Booking links let people pick a time with you. See Booking links.
Tip

Names spelled wrong in transcripts? Add them to your word list in Dictate → Words. Meetings uses the same list.

What it needs

A Deepgram key for transcripts and an Anthropic key for the notes, added by an admin. Connect Google Calendar for attendee names and reminders.
