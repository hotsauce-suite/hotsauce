Background music

Hotsauce can play calm music while you work: soft piano, lo-fi, ambient or rain. It is off until you turn it on, and only you hear it.

Turn it on

  1. On a computer, click your initials (or your photo) at the bottom of the left bar, then Music. On a phone, tap Apps, then Music.
  2. Press the play button. Pick a track and set the volume with the slider. On an iPhone or iPad, use the device’s volume buttons instead.
  3. Press it again to pause. The note shows a dot while music is on.

New to Hotsauce? The Getting started tile on Today asks once whether you’d like music. Press Turn on or No thanks.

The music keeps playing as you move between apps. Hotsauce remembers your choice, track and volume on that device. After you reload the page, it starts again at your first click, because browsers don’t let a page play sound on its own.

It pauses while you record

When Hotsauce opens your microphone, camera or screen (meeting notes, Dictate, voice notes, Ramble or a Clip), the music fades out so it never ends up in a recording. It comes back when you stop.

The Mac app’s own dictation and call recording run outside the page, so the music doesn’t pause for those. Use headphones if you want to keep it playing then.

What it needs

Nothing. The tracks ship with Hotsauce and load only when you press play. They aren’t kept for offline use.
