Help / The apps

Dictate and voice notes

Dictate turns speech into clean text. On a Mac, hold a key, talk, and the text lands wherever your cursor is. On a phone, record a voice note and get back a title, summary and to-dos.

Dictate on a Mac

  1. Get the Mac app and connect it to your account.
  2. Click into any app: Slack, Mail, a doc, the terminal.
  3. Hold the right Option key and talk. Let go, and clean, punctuated text appears. (You can pick another key under Hotkey in the menu bar bottle.)

Dictate drops the “ums” and fixes corrections like “Tuesday, no wait, Wednesday”.

While it listens, a warm glow lines the edges of the window you’re talking into, with flames along the bottom that rise and fall with your voice. It settles to a slow pulse while your words are written up and fades once the text is in. Turn it off under Edge glow while dictating in the Mac app’s Settings → Dictation. With Reduce motion on in your Mac’s Accessibility settings, the glow stays still, with no flames.

With no connection

On a plane or with the Wi-Fi off, the Mac app still types what you say. It uses your Mac’s own speech recognition, and the pill at the bottom of the screen says Offline: on-device. Nothing is sent anywhere: the audio stays on your Mac. The text is what you said, with punctuation, but without the clean-up and your word list, which need the connection. These dictations are saved in your history marked on this Mac.

  • The first time, your Mac asks to allow Speech Recognition for Hotsauce. Press Allow. If you said no, turn it on in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Speech Recognition.
  • It speaks your Mac’s language, or US English if your Mac has no offline dictation for it yet. To add your language, turn on Dictation in System Settings → Keyboard and pick it there.
  • If Hotsauce stops answering in the middle of a dictation, what you said is written up on your Mac instead, so you don’t lose it.

Models

Open Dictate → Models to see what hears you and what tidies your words, for example Speech: Deepgram Nova-3 · Offline: Apple, on the Mac · Cleanup: Claude Haiku 5.5. Pick how your dictations are tidied:

  • Fast, the default: quick, and right almost every time.
  • Careful: a bigger model, better with names and long rambles, about a second slower.
  • Off: your exact words, as heard.

Your choice is yours alone and follows you to every Mac and the browser. The Mac app’s Literal mode still skips the tidying on that Mac.

Word list

Open Dictate → Words to add names and terms it should spell your way. There’s a personal list and a shared team list, and Meetings uses them too.

Voice notes on your phone

  1. Add Hotsauce to your home screen. Opened from there, it starts on voice notes.
  2. Tap record and talk, for as long as you need.
  3. You get back a title, a summary and any to-dos, saved in Dictate → Voice notes.
What it needs

A Deepgram key for speech, and an Anthropic key for the clean-up and summaries.
