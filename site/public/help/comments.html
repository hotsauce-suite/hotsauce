Comments and mentions

Every team doc, meeting note and task has a conversation under it. Ask a question where the work is, and @mention a teammate so they hear about it and find it in their For you list.

Try it in the live demo →Sample data, nothing is saved.

Add a comment

  1. Open a doc or meeting note in the team space and scroll to the end, or press the speech-bubble button at the top. On a team task, the comments are at the bottom of the task.
  2. Type in the box. Enter starts a new line.
  3. Press Comment (⌘ Enter works). Teammates who have it open see your comment appear.

Comments are plain text. Links open, and line breaks stay where you put them. A comment is never part of the doc’s own text.

Mention someone

  1. Type @ and the first letters of a name.
  2. Pick the person from the list with the arrow keys and Enter, or click them.
  3. Send the comment. They get one notification on each device where they turned notifications on, and opening it lands on your comment.

The name is highlighted once the comment is sent, so you can see who was told. If you edit a comment and add a new name, only the new person is notified. Mentioning yourself sends nothing.

For you

For you lists every place someone @mentioned you, newest first. It sits on the rail right under Today, with a number for the ones you haven’t opened, and as a tile on Today. On a phone, the number is on For you if you put it on the bar at the bottom, otherwise on the Today button, otherwise as a dot on Apps.

  1. Click an entry to open the doc, meeting note or task at that comment. It stays on the list, but no longer counts.
  2. Press Done to take it off the list. If someone mentions you there again, it comes back at the top. Right after Done, Undo puts it back.
  3. Press Later and pick tomorrow or next Monday. It disappears until 9 AM that day in your time zone, then comes back at the top and counts again. A new mention brings it back sooner.

What you’ve opened, finished or put off is kept in your personal space, so it’s the same on every device and nobody else sees it. Replies in conversations you’re part of and tasks assigned to you will join the list in a later release.

Comment on a passage

In a doc or meeting note, select some text and press Comment beside it. The passage is quoted above your comment, so everyone knows which part you mean.

Edit or delete

You can edit or delete your own comments. An edited comment says “edited”. An admin can delete anyone’s comment.

Good to know

  • Team space only. Docs, notes and tasks in your personal space have no comments, because nobody else can open them. A team task moved to your personal space leaves its conversation behind.
  • Everyone on the team can read them. A comment is as visible as the doc it sits under.
  • They follow the doc. Rename or move a doc and its conversation comes along. Delete it and the conversation goes too.
  • You need a connection. Without one the box says so and keeps what you typed until you’re back.
  • Long conversations open on the most recent comments (oldest at the top, newest at the bottom), with the earlier ones a click away.
What it needs

Nothing extra. To be told about mentions, turn on notifications in Settings on each device.
