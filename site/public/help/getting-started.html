Getting started

Hotsauce is your team’s office suite: docs, meeting notes, tasks, files and more, in one place with one sign-in. Here’s the five-minute tour.

Sign in

  1. Open the address your admin sent you, like https://hotsauce.yourcompany.com.
  2. Choose Continue with Google and pick your work account. Only email addresses from your company’s domains can get in.
  3. Invited from outside the company? Sign in with the exact email address you were invited with, and Hotsauce emails you a code.

Find your way around

  • The left bar on a computer lists every app. Hover an icon to see its name. Your picture at the bottom opens your account menu and Settings.
  • On a phone, the bar at the bottom holds Today and your most-used apps. Apps opens the rest.
  • Today is your home page. It pulls your meetings, tasks and changes from the other apps into one view. See Today.
  • Search with ⌘ K (or Ctrl K on Windows) to find any doc, meeting, transcript or task by its title or the words inside it.

Team space and your own space

Most apps have two places to keep things. Team is shared with everyone in your organization. My (your personal space) is only for you: nobody else can open it, admins included. You can move things from one to the other.

Where your work is saved

Docs, meeting notes, tasks, notes, tables and links are saved as plain text files with a full history of every change. You can always see who changed what, and bring back an older version. Your organization owns those files outright and can take them anywhere.

Light or dark

Hotsauce follows your device’s light or dark setting. To pick one for this device, open Settings and choose under Appearance.

Something not working?

If your admin turned it on, Send feedback in the app sends a bug report or idea straight to the people who run your Hotsauce. You can also read the FAQ.
