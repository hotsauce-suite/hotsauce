Help / Other devices and agents

Screenshots

The Mac app takes screenshots with one shortcut. The picture is on your clipboard at once, you can mark it up, and Share turns it into a link.

Take a screenshot

  1. Press ⌥ ⇧ 4 anywhere on your Mac. The screen dims and the pointer shows the size in pixels.
  2. Drag to capture an area, click a window to capture that window, or click the desktop to capture the whole screen. Esc cancels.
  3. The picture is copied to your clipboard and saved in Pictures → Hotsauce Screenshots. Paste it anywhere with ⌘ V.

The first time, macOS asks to let Hotsauce record the screen. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording, switch on Hotsauce, then quit Hotsauce and open it again.

The preview in the corner

After each shot a small preview floats in the corner for about 7 seconds, and stays while your pointer is on it.

  • Edit opens the editor.
  • Copy copies the picture again.
  • Share uploads it and copies a link.
  • Pin keeps the preview on screen until you close it.
  • Drag the preview into any app to drop the picture there.

Mark it up

The editor has arrows, rectangles, ellipses, text, a highlighter, numbered steps, redaction and crop. Everything you add stays movable and can be undone with ⌘ Z.

  • Redact covers what’s underneath with a solid block. Once you copy, save or share, the covered part is gone from the picture for good.
  • Copy Text copies the words in the picture, skipping anything you redacted.
  • Save (⌘ S) writes your changes over the screenshot’s file. Closing the editor or quitting saves them too.
  • File → Edit Last Screenshot and Recent Screenshots reopen your last five shots, still editable, until you quit the app.

Share a link

Screenshots stay on your Mac until you press Share. Share uploads that one picture to your Files, in the Screenshots folder of your personal space, and copies a link to it.

  • Anyone with the link can open it, with no account, when your admin allows links for people outside (see Share links and pages). Otherwise the link works for signed-in teammates, and the app tells you so.
  • A shared screenshot and its link are deleted 30 days after you shared it. To keep one, open it in Files and press Keep.
  • The copy on your Mac is never deleted for you.
  • You need to be signed in to Hotsauce in the Mac app’s window to share.

Change the shortcut and the folder

Open Settings → This Mac in the Mac app (⌘ ,) and find Screenshots: record another shortcut, choose where shots are saved, and pick what happens after a capture (copy it, save it, show the preview).
