Help / The apps

Tasks

Tasks is your to-do list and your team’s. Type a task the way you’d say it, and Hotsauce sets the date, priority and project for you.

Try it in the live demo →Sample data, nothing is saved.

Quick add

Press q or click Add task, then type:

Send the deck fri p1 #Launch @calls +sam
  • Dates: today, tomorrow, fri, next week, nov 20 3pm.
  • Repeats: every friday, every weekday, every month. When you complete it, it moves to the next date.
  • Priority: p1 (most urgent) to p3.
  • Project: #Launch. Label: @calls. Assign: +sam.

Ramble

Press Ramble and talk through everything on your mind for up to five minutes. Hotsauce lists the tasks it heard, and you pick the ones to keep.

Views

Inbox, Today, Upcoming, Projects and Done. Drag to reorder. Team tasks can be assigned and can carry files. You get a reminder on your phone or computer when a task is due, once notifications are on for that device (tick Remind me a minute before in Meetings).

Tip

Coming from Todoist? Import from Todoist is in the Tasks sidebar.

What it needs

Ramble needs Deepgram and Anthropic keys. Everything else works out of the box.
