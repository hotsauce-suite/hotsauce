Ask: the AI assistant

The assistant knows what’s in your workspace: your docs, meetings, tasks, notes, tables, clips, links and calendar. Ask it a question, or ask it to do something. It sees only what you can see.

Coming in the next release. This page describes features being built now. They’ll work once your Hotsauce updates.

Open it

  • Press ⌘ J, or click Ask in the top bar. It’s named after your workspace, like “Ask Hotsauce”.
  • In ⌘ K, type your question and choose Ask the assistant.

It opens as a panel beside whatever you’re working on, on any page.

It knows what you’re looking at

When a doc or meeting is open, a small chip in the panel shows it, so “summarize this” or “turn this into tasks” just works. Remove the chip if you want a question about everything instead.

Things to ask

  • “Prep me for my next meeting.”
  • “What did we decide about pricing last month?”
  • “Turn the action items from this meeting into tasks for me.”
  • “Draft a project brief from these notes in a new doc.”
  • “Which of my tasks are overdue?”

When it changes things

  • Making or editing a doc, task or note happens right away, with an Undo button in the chat. Every change is saved in history, under your name.
  • Deleting or moving a file always asks you to press Confirm first.
  • It only acts when you ask. It doesn’t search the internet: its answers come from your workspace.

Your conversations

Past conversations are saved in your personal space, visible only to you, and kept out of search. Delete any of them whenever you like.

What it needs

An Anthropic key, added by an admin. Admins can see each person’s usage this month in Settings, and can set a monthly limit.
