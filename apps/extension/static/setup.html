Taking notes on a call

  1. Join the call in Google Meet. The icon shows a small dot.
  2. Click the icon, then Take notes. Hotsauce opens beside the call.
  3. Click Start recording. Type your own notes if you like.
  4. Click Stop when the call ends. The written notes follow a minute later.

On any other page, the same icon saves the page to Links. No bot joins your calls.
